Our story

It started as the coffee we made for our own late nights.

Code Bear began as the coffee we brewed for long build sessions: strong, smooth, and easy to reach for at two in the morning. Every bag is named after a moment anyone who writes code has lived through.


We roast small batches every week and keep the list short on purpose, so each coffee gets our full attention.

How we roast.

No secrets, just care and a notebook.
